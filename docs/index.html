Attune

Project 01: Attune

Attune is creating a scalable form of neurofeedback therapy that is accessible to both patients and mental health professionals, provides insights into the user's mental state, and is genuinely fun and beneficial for the patient to use.

Neurofeedback EEG Virtual Reality ADHD

Project Managers: Rushil Shah; Addison Torgerson

Searchable details

Current work: VR-EEG headset proof of concept; focus-based game proof of concept; Cozad New Venture Challenge 2025 and 2026; Undergraduate Research Symposium 2025; NSF I-Corps Workshop Series 2026

Resources: Meta Quest 2; existing game codebase; EnterpriseWorks Designer-in-Residence; VR-EEG hardware; clinical and business-development contacts

Goals: Refine the proofs of concept into full prototypes; pursue Cozad funding and outside investment; file a provisional patent; begin pilot studies with clinics; work toward an FDA-approved neurofeedback device

Keywords: neurofeedback therapy ADHD attention focus mental health VR virtual reality EEG BCI headset game FDA medical device startup clinic pilot study patent Cozad I-Corps

project04 Research

MindScents

Project 04: MindScents

MindScents is developing and validating an EEG-based system that combines controlled olfactory stimulation, physiological signal processing, and machine learning to model how scents and concentrations influence emotional states.

EEG Olfaction Emotion Olfactometer

Project Manager: Amity Zhang

Searchable details

Current work: Protocol refinement and early prototype development; olfactometer design; EEG preprocessing pipeline; IRB preparation; faculty coordination; funding applications; EOH and Cozad presentations

Resources: 16-channel EEG/BCI system; Python analysis pipeline; SEED-OLF benchmark dataset; project proposals and pitch decks; Prof. Catherine Best-Popescu; Prof. Florin Dolcos; NeuroTech@UIUC; EOH and Cozad feedback

Goals: Build and validate an EEG-synchronized, computer-controlled olfactometer; collect an IRB-approved pilot dataset from at least 10 participants across three scents and three concentrations; evaluate subject-independent emotion decoding

Keywords: EEG scent smell olfaction emotion affect concentration olfactometer physiological signal processing machine learning SEED-OLF transfer learning IRB pilot study personalized fragrance EOH Cozad
